WHO WE ARE

Your Gateway to Chinese Excellence

LensTalk Education is composed of a team of professional, experienced, and passionate tutors, who have helped more than 2000 international students all over the world to master Chinese and achieve their language goals. Our innovative teaching methods, remarkable instructors, and immersive cultural programs set us apart.

We believe that language learning goes beyond textbooks, we also believe that everyone is unique, the student experience and the quality of coaching are far more important than the number of students. That's why we are dedicated in One on One learning mode, and provide customized and personalized guidance, ensuring our students not only learn how to use Chinese and achieve their language goals, but also truly understand Chinese life and culture.

Lens Talk, let's talk! We sincerely invite you to join us, experience firsthand the beauty of the Chinese language and culture, and make every day count.
