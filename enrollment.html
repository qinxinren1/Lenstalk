Requirements of Apply Class

Academic Requirements

  • No previous Chinese knowledge required
  • Master basic English for communication
  • Students under the age of 16 need to be accompanied by their parents or guardians

Required Documents

  • Passport copy (valid for 6+ months)
  • For information on applying for a Chinese tourist visa, please contact the local visa center. Any special materials required for visa application can be sent to us via email, we will provide relevant supporting materials
  • Personal identification photo × 2

Application Timeline

  • Apply at least one month before start date
  • Processing time (Including visa applications, course and tutor arrangements, and international payments): 2-4 weeks
  • Visa application: 3 to 6 business days
  • Arrival: 1 day before classes

Classes are held every Monday to Friday, so you'd better arrive Kunming at certain Saturday or Sunday, then you can start your program immediately afterwards at next Monday.
