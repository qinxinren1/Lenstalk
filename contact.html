Frequently Asked Questions

Will there be a pick-up service?

We will offer airport pick-up and drop-off services. The service fee has been included in your tuition fee.

I'm a vegetarian. Can I find suitable meals?

Of course, we will take into account different dietary requirements and recommend delicious meals that suitable.

Can I use cash for shopping in China?

Some shopping malls and stores in China can accept cash now, but Chinese people are more accustomed to using mobile payment. Don't worry. We will assist you in applying for WeChat payment and Alipay payment.

Do I need the textbook?

We will recommend textbooks suitable for your Chinese proficiency level. You can choose to purchase them or use the free electronic versions.

Can I choose my own hotel for accommodation?

We will provide accommodation and do our best to meet the students' requirements. However, if you really need other accommodation options, you can choose them yourself, but the cost will be your responsibility.

Can I travel by myself during my free time?

Sure, we will also recommend you to visit wonderful attractions and try our best to offer travel advice and assistance.
